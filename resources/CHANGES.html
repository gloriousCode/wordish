Changes

Wordish 1.6.0 is prepared locally and has not yet been published. It retains the existing app UUID and saved settings.

version 1.6.0 (unreleased)

  • Renames Fuzzy Text Two to Wordish.
  • Smooths large clock fonts on Time 2 and Round 2, with measured bounds to keep complete words inside the screen.
  • Replaces the colourful presets with Lagoon, Ember, Orchid, Grove and Sand: coordinated palettes with clearer word and date contrast.
  • Adds animation and double-tap switches, adjustable exact-time duration and optional silent battery/connection indicators.
  • Improves settings preview, colour migration and delivery retries.
  • Fixes truncated translations, missing digital-time glyphs, buffer bounds and interrupted animation cleanup.

version 1.4.10

  • Adds a double tap gesture that briefly shows the exact digital time before returning to fuzzy text.

version 1.4.9

  • Adds a preset selector with quiet, mid-range, and colourful styles that demonstrate row colours, date section colours, date placement, and date formats.
  • Replaces the corner date radio groups with labelled placement and format selectors.

version 1.4.8

  • Adds optional separate colours for minute row one, minute row two, hour row, and each corner date section.

version 1.4.7

  • Adds an optional small corner date with top or bottom placement and compact numeric or abbreviated formats.

version 1.2.1

  • Version 1.2.0 was released with the wrong settings URL; this version fixes that.

version 1.2.0

  • Fixes blank settings page on iOS devices. (Many thanks to Tomi De Lucca!)
  • Adds translation, English (Great Britain).
  • Fixes for Spanish translation. (Again, thanks to Tomi De Lucca!)
